## Six decisions: recommendations and boundaries

- **D-PIN — DRAFT/BLOCKED.** Recommend separate Owner approval of a source/repository digest and scoped order/lifecycle selection, then atomically recheck current authority, revision and claim/fence at first admission while committing immutable pin and run/work/authority/active together. Later attempts and holds retain that pin; execute or resume only verified snapshot bytes. Example: orders A and B on the same source choose X and Y; if selection changes X→Y before A's admission, stale X must fail without partial rows, while admission committed first preserves X. This protects B1 authority, receipts and accepted holds and supports M9 multi-pack/resume. Unresolved: actual selection capability/API, registry/revision and durable representation, transaction and race order, retry actor/current authority, revoke/regrant and hold behavior, host routing and error precedence. **Rows:** P01/P01b/P02/P03/P03b for authority and atomic race; P04–P06/P09 for snapshot, cross-binding and retry. Evidence must include real product/store boundary and connection/process races; a config digest alone is not per-order selection or proof of replacement safety. [Candidate](b2-w0-converged-candidate.md); [matrix](b2-acceptance-matrix-draft.md); [plan v1](2026-10-28_214327-b2-product-execution-plan.md).
- **D-LEGACY — DRAFT/BLOCKED.** Recommend preserve original bytes and distinguish authorized read, exact receipt retry, accepted continuation and new activation. Only a single digest with complete original authority and verified original snapshot is a candidate for continuation after current authority/fence checks; reject mixed or incomplete continuation and do not invent a pin for new activation. Example: a mixed-digest history remains readable under existing scope but must not be silently backfilled into a new execution. This conservative policy can conflict with B1 accepted continuation, so that compatibility must be decided, including collection of an already accepted stage after revoke. Unresolved: actual history census, provenance, each combination's policy, old open connections and new opens, quiescence/fence, WAL backup, crash and recovery/rollback. **Rows:** P07/P08, R01/R02; synthetic combinations are not a production census, and a matching-digest guard is not proof that an old writer cannot start a stage. Restore after new durable writes requires reconciliation, not a lossless rollback claim. [Candidate](b2-w0-converged-candidate.md); [matrix](b2-acceptance-matrix-draft.md).
- **D-TASK — DRAFT/BLOCKED.** Recommend retain immutable first issue snapshot and raw digest; bind a separate canonical task and digest to source/repository/order/lifecycle/admission, explicitly authorized by an Owner for execution. DriveReview consumes that authority; human approval of results remains separate. Example: editing admitted issue A into B must neither replace A nor execute B; unsupported A edited into valid-looking B must not auto-execute. This preserves B1 intake/receipt and M9's actual execution requirement without treating issue text as a command. Unresolved: supported grammar and exact bytes/limits, `[dependencies]` versus workspace dependencies, canonicalization and duplicate keys, repository/OID identity, inspector/lockfile and offline failure, authorization API/idempotency, ready transition, result/approval binding and error precedence. **Rows:** E02/E02b negative and edit cases; A01/E01 delivery and result binding; E03 revocation/fence. A fixed fake task does not establish the general task grammar or real Die behavior. [Candidate](b2-w0-converged-candidate.md); [matrix](b2-acceptance-matrix-draft.md).
- **D-PRODUCER — DRAFT/BLOCKED.** Recommend demonstrate a single approved pack and explicit fixed-task authorization through the real product entrypoint, ready, claim/fence, test fake producer, S1, host and separate finalizer before W1 relies on the seam. Example: a P2 helper that directly promotes ready or injects S1 must not stand in for producer invocation. This preserves B1 transaction/hold boundaries and supports M9 product execution, but proves neither actual Die nor human approval. Unresolved: entrypoint/assembly, ports/DTO/files and dependency direction, authorization and producer records, delegated bridge authority, `review`/`inspecting` mismatch, Passed-to-waiting transition, stage evidence, revoke, crash/retry and finalizer behavior. **Rows:** A01 for the seam, P02 for two packs, E01 separately for real Die and human approval. Static watch-path searches do not prove all dynamic paths absent. [Candidate](b2-w0-converged-candidate.md); [bridge conflict](b2-w0-a01-bridge-candidate.md).
- **D-ISOLATION — DRAFT/BLOCKED.** Recommend retain serve orchestration and measure per-order execution isolation and authenticated run/provider/budget proxy before untrusted task execution by real Die or code-executing inspector. Example: prove a test secret and DB/WAL exist and are reachable from the trusted side, then show the execution boundary cannot read them while its own workspace and authorized proxy work. Same UID or environment cleanup is not file isolation. This preserves B1 authority and meets M9 actual negative/positive runtime checks. Unresolved: image and launcher, UID/GID, mounts, network/DNS/redirect and IPv4/IPv6, secrets/daemon trust, proxy listener and budget, child termination, safe local Docker fixture and exact pre-E01 subset of I02–I06. **Rows:** I01–I06, with the agreed subset prerequisite to E01; W2 precondition is not W3 deployment PASS. Rendered config, mocks and absent targets do not prove runtime denial. [Candidate](b2-w0-converged-candidate.md); [M9](../../.specs/m9-plan.md).
- **D-TRACE — DRAFT/BLOCKED.** Recommend map every existing row to its inputs, positive/negative assertions, actual file/function, command, observation layer, evidence location, B1/M9 obligation and approved file/schema/wire scope, followed by narrow independent verification. Example: a fake producer can support A01 only at its declared boundary; it cannot turn E01 or I02 into PASS. Unresolved: exact executable tests and commands, all assertions/evidence locations, compound failure precedence and scope decisions. **Rows:** all A/P/E/I/R rows above. Only complete evidence at the required layer supports PASS; missing execution is NOT-RUN/NOT-PERFORMED and conflicting results FAIL. B1 regression and all M9 requirements remain intact. [Candidate](b2-w0-converged-candidate.md); [matrix rules](b2-acceptance-matrix-draft.md).

## W0 exit versus proposed deferral

The supplied reviewer log is an **untrusted report**, not authority: it recommends freezing semantic policy and transaction boundaries in W0 and deferring exact filenames/commands, complete grammar, isolation plumbing and migration implementation to later work (`deleg_0907d1b4/task-1`; transcript not included in this repository). That is a **proposed scope change, not approved**. It conflicts with the current draft W0 closure requirements: the matrix requires actual APIs/schema candidates and exact row files/commands/assertions; legacy writer/backup/rollback and real old-binary fixture; one complete grammar, bytes, limits and examples; producer ports/DTO/files; implementable isolation and safe Docker conditions; all rows' detailed evidence and independent review ([matrix](b2-acceptance-matrix-draft.md), [exit](b2-acceptance-matrix-draft.md)). The reviewer cannot override these requirements. Plan v1 also requires a precise row rubric and W0 evidence ([plan v1](2026-10-28_214327-b2-product-execution-plan.md)). No existing draft approval is assumed.

Under the current requirements, all of those items must be frozen and independently reviewed **before W0 closure**, including approved scope and W0 commit; the per-stage list below is a dependency checklist, not permission to move current W0 obligations later. If an authoritative human explicitly approves a revised W0 scope, that change must identify the exact deferred requirements and preserve the following gates:

1. **Before W2a implementation:** freeze its precise fixed-task bytes and authorization, product entrypoint and ports/DTOs/files, identity and transaction/receipt/fence/bridge authority, Passed transition and separate finalizer, B1 compatibility, exact A01 test/command/assertions/evidence, crash and retry boundaries, and any necessary authorized file/schema/wire exception. If schema changes, freeze migration and old-writer safety first. A fake only handles the fixed test input; it does not authorize general input or code execution.
2. **Before W1 implementation:** freeze selection/registry authority, revision CAS, admission pin atomicity, snapshot/resume, legacy read/retry/continuation/new work, error precedence, race and real old-writer fixtures, migration/backup/recovery, P-row tests and evidence and exact scope.
3. **Before W2 implementation and especially real Die or code-executing inspection:** freeze general grammar, byte framing/limits, authority and result binding, failure policy, E-row evidence and scope; before execution, demonstrate the agreed isolation/proxy subset with effective identity/mount/network boundary, positive controls, denial and child control. A fixture or probe that executes untrusted code needs that protection too.
4. **Before W3 implementation/acceptance:** freeze deploy and override policy, safe Docker resources/local-image/no-pull conditions, actual listener/runtime positive and negative I-row tests and commands, and required scope. **Before W4:** freeze exact B1/M9 regression commands, R-row evidence/scope audit and independent verification. Each W ends only after its own required evidence, W-level commit and stop/report, not a draft or earlier stage PASS ([matrix](b2-acceptance-matrix-draft.md); [plan](2026-10-28_214327-b2-product-execution-plan.md)).

Safety-critical items cannot be deferred past the effect they protect: before any schema migration or new durable writes decide upgrade compatibility, old **already-open** and newly opened writer fencing, quiescence, WAL-consistent backup, crash/uncertain commit and restore/reconciliation, receipt/accepted-result preservation and applicable authorization; before untrusted execution establish task authority, input boundary and measured effective isolation/proxy controls. A version check on new open alone does not fence an old connection. Operational DB or remote effects require separate explicit human scope authority; this packet grants none. [Legacy candidate](b2-w0-converged-candidate.md); [plan v1](2026-10-28_214327-b2-product-execution-plan.md).
